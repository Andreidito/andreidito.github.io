Resolving Suspended Sediment Concentration Profiles in Uniform Open-Channel Flow with a 1DV Model

1DV numerical modelling · MATLAB · Sediment transport · August 2026

An individual study of how a vertical one-dimensional model represents suspended sediment concentration through the water column in steady, uniform open-channel flow.

Study

The dissertation investigates numerical representation of suspended sediment concentration profiles, including the role of turbulent mixing and sediment diffusion. It uses experimental measurements from 17 runs reported by Cellino (1998).

Approach

I implemented and calibrated a vertical one-dimensional (1DV) solver in MATLAB. Four sediment-diffusion formulations were compared with the Rouse reference solution and the experimental measurements. I consolidated the model and measurement comparisons in an Excel workbook.

My individual work
This was my master's dissertation, completed individually under the supervision of Prof. Erik A. Toorman.

Key topics

  • One-dimensional vertical numerical modelling of suspended sediment.
  • MATLAB implementation and calibration.
  • Comparison of four diffusion formulations with Rouse theory and 17 experimental runs.
  • Excel workbook for consolidating model and measurement comparisons.
